# Interview Questions for Junior Roles in Semiconductor Industry

## Overview

A curated repository of technical interview questions and conceptual focus areas faced by the NUST College of E&ME (Class of 2026) during hiring cycles for ASIC, FPGA, RTL Design, and Embedded Systems roles across the Pakistani semiconductor and design services ecosystem.

This repository serves as a comprehensive resource for students and professionals preparing for technical interviews in semiconductor, FPGA design, RTL design, and embedded systems positions.
